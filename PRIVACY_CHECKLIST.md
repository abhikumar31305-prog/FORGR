# Privacy and Compliance Checklist

Before public launch, obtain the institution's legal/privacy approval for:

- Purpose limitation and consent for student analytics, resumes, email, and risk predictions.
- Role-based access and least privilege for students, parents, faculty, recruiters, placement staff, and admins.
- Retention and deletion schedules for source CSVs, resumes, predictions, audit logs, and backups.
- Student access, correction, export, and deletion requests.
- Processor/vendor agreements for hosting, email, payment, monitoring, and object storage.
- Encryption in transit and at rest, secret management, breach response, and backup access.
- Human review and appeal process for risk and placement recommendations.
- Public privacy notice, cookie/session disclosure, and support contact.
